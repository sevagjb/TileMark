Link check

Finds links that no longer work and lists them under "Broken links". A link counts as broken when its address cannot be reached twice in a row, or the site says the page is gone.

Backup

One file with all your links, groups, their order and the thumbnails. Give it a password to encrypt it.

Restore

Replaces all your current links and groups with the ones from a backup file (a backup from LinkBoard 1.0 works too). Your account and other people's links stay as they are.
