Everything your bookmarks bar never was

One small app, one container, one data folder. No cloud service, no tracking, no account anywhere.

🖼️

Links you can see

Every link gets the picture and title its site publishes. Find things at a glance instead of reading a list of names.

🗂️

Organised your way

Coloured groups in your own order, favourites on top, notes on cards, and two layouts to choose from.

📲

Save from anywhere

A browser button, your phone's Share menu, or an API key for scripts and Shortcuts.

🔒

Private for each person

Everyone in your home gets their own links. Two-factor sign-in, device list, and nothing reachable without signing in.

🧹

Tidy by itself

Duplicates are recognised, tracking junk is stripped from addresses, and a weekly check finds links that stopped working.

🏠

Up and running in five minutes

Pick the way you run your home server.

Docker

Dockge, Portainer or plain Docker Compose:

services:
  tilemark:
    image: ghcr.io/sevagjb/tilemark:latest
    container_name: tilemark
    restart: unless-stopped
    ports:
      - "8080:8080"
    volumes:
      - ./data:/data
